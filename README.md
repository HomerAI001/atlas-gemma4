# What is Atlas?

Atlas is an AI companion built for people systematically failed by mainstream mental health AI. Standard safety implementations, trained on neurotypical response preferences via RLHF, produce crisis escalation responses that are actively harmful to people with complex trauma, CPTSD, and neurodivergent profiles.

Atlas replaces this with a compound architecture that separates intelligence from safety classification, allowing the model to hold space, witness, and preserve agency without pathologising.

## Core Principles

- **Agency-preserving witness**: not redirect, not pathologise
- **Critical-tier protocol**: crisis resources offered as a choice, never a reflex
- **No restoration promise**: meets the user as they are today
- **Sovereign data architecture**: architectural incapability of data exposure, not contractual prohibition

## Theoretical Foundation

Atlas is supported by the **Omega Relational Trauma Index (Omega RTI)** framework — a seven-axis tensor engine (REL, OCCF, EREG, CC, SOM, VIC, NI) that provides continuous emotional state tracking across sessions.

## Key Evidence

RLHF is a mean-fitting process that systematically disadvantages outlier populations. Constitutional AI (Bai et al. 2022, arXiv:2212.08073) produces crisis escalation responses that are harmful to the target cohort . Atlas demonstrates that separating intelligence from safety classification eliminates these false positives while maintaining genuine safety (Suhas et al 2026, arXiv:2604.23445).
